A scholion is a book.scholion reference in an edition of the scholia, whether or not it has text; lemma and comment are one scholion. A scholion counts as indexed if the commentary index (urn:cite2:hmt:commentary.v1:all) has an entry for it naming a passage of the Iliad. Scholia are assigned to the manuscript whose Iliad version identifier begins their own (msAint belongs to msA). Pages are the surfaces of DSE records. A scholion is on the same page as its Iliad passage if the two share at least one page; a range of lines is on every page of its lines. Entries are compared only where both have DSE records and the Iliad passage is in the same manuscript. Findings are warnings only.
