    A scholion is listed here if the editions have it and no DSE record names it (its lemma and comment are one passage; subreferences are ignored). The book is the book of the Iliad passage the index of scholia says it comments on, not the number in the scholion's own reference; a scholion that comments on passages of several books is listed under each of them, and one the index does not place is under "no book". Hover over (or focus) a reference to read the scholion; click it to copy its diplomatic text. Findings are warnings only.
