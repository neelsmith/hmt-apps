A line of the Iliad counts as edited if its passage (book.line) has text in either the diplomatic or the normalized exemplar; book titles are not counted. A scholion is a numbered scholion (book.scholion) with a lemma or comment in either exemplar; all groups of scholia of a manuscript (for the Venetus A: main, interior, exterior, interlinear, intermarginal and others) are added together. Only manuscripts with edited lines, and codices with at least one page in a DSE record, are shown. A page counts if any DSE record names it as its surface, whatever the text it is linked to (Iliad lines or scholia). Findings are warnings only.
