By text

Passages are compared by version: a line counts as in the DSE if any record names it, in either exemplar (diplomatic or normalized); a scholion's lemma and comment are one passage. Subreferences (1.1@word) are ignored. Physical surfaces (pages) are not checked here. Findings are warnings only.
